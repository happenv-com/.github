## Who we are

[Happenv](https://happenv.com) is a software development company specializing in e-commerce solutions, logistics systems and Order Management Systems (OMS). We design, build and maintain scalable business applications that help companies streamline operations, automate workflows and improve customer experiences.

Our expertise includes custom development, system integrations and long-term support of solutions built with **Laravel** and **Filament**, delivering reliable and efficient platforms tailored to modern commerce and logistics needs.

| | |
|---|---|
| **E-commerce & OMS** | Order management, marketplace integrations, stock and returns in one flow. |
| **Logistics** | Shipping, labels and parcel tracking across couriers. |
| **Custom development** | Business applications designed around the way your team works. |
| **Integrations** | Marketplaces, shops, couriers, invoicing and accounting systems. |
| **Long-term support** | We maintain what we build — updates, monitoring and new features. |

### Laravel packages

| Package | What it does |
|---|---|
| [**Laravel True Modular**](https://github.com/happenv-com/laravel-true-modular) | Makes a modular monolith explicit, deterministic and analyzable — topological service provider ordering and an enhanced application lifecycle. |
